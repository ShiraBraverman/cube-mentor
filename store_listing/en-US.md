## App name
CubeMentor: 3D Cube Solver

## Short description
Scan any 2x2 to 6x6 cube, solve it step by step in 3D and learn to solve it too

## Full description
Stuck on a scrambled cube? Point your camera at it and CubeMentor shows you how to solve it, move by move, on an interactive 3D cube. Then learn to do it yourself with a full beginner-to-advanced Academy. Works offline, no account needed.

🧩 SOLVE ANY CUBE FROM 2x2 TO 6x6
• Camera scanner reads the colors of all six faces on your device, with tap-to-fix for any sticker
• Prefer typing? Enter the colors manually on a flat cube map
• Smart validation catches impossible cubes before you start
• Supports 2x2 Pocket Cube, 3x3, 4x4 Revenge, 5x5 Professor and 6x6
• Western or Japanese color scheme

⚡ TWO WAYS TO SOLVE
• Fast Solve: the shortest route for 2x2 and 3x3, solved in milliseconds
• Guided Solve: the same layer-by-layer method you learn in the Academy, broken into clear stages so you understand every move
• 4x4, 5x5 and 6x6: reduction method (centers, edge pairing, 3x3 stage) with OLL and PLL parity fixes

🎮 INTERACTIVE 3D CUBE
• Smooth animated turns, including wide and inner-slice moves
• Play, pause, step forward and back, and change the speed
• Rotate the cube with your finger to see it from any side
• Standard notation (R, U', Rw, 2R…) shown for every move

🎓 29-LESSON CUBE ACADEMY
• Beginner 3x3 course: white cross, first layer, middle layer, yellow cross and last layer, one lesson per step
• Each lesson explains the goal, how to hold the cube and why the algorithm works
• Practice every algorithm on a live 3D cube
• 2x2 lessons plus 4x4, 5x5 and 6x6 lessons covering centers, edge pairing and parity
• Track your progress across every cube size

✨ 3D PATTERN PLAYGROUND
• Browse beautiful cube patterns like checkerboard, cube-in-a-cube and more
• Watch each pattern being made in 3D and copy it on your own cube
• Free-play 3D cube to turn and experiment

⏱️ SPEEDCUBING TOOLS
• Speed timer with WCA-style 15-second inspection and random scrambles
• Daily Challenge: one new scramble every day, your streak and your best time

🔒 PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• No account, no sign-up, no subscription
• Scanning, solving, lessons, timer and daily challenge all work without internet
• Camera images are processed on your device and never uploaded
• Your progress and times stay on your phone

🌍 22 LANGUAGES
English, Hebrew, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), French, German, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Filipino, Hindi, Bengali, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Vietnamese, Burmese and Tatar.

Whether you are solving your first cube or chasing a faster time on the 5x5, CubeMentor is your personal cube coach. Download it and solve your cube today!

CubeMentor is free with ads. Some lessons, patterns and big-cube solves can be unlocked for 24 hours by watching a short video.
