# CubeMentor Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

CubeMentor ("the app", "we", "us") is an offline cube solver and learning
app for 2x2 to 6x6 puzzles. This policy explains what information the app
handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the CubeMentor apps
on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

## The short version

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, login or subscription, and we run
  no servers that receive your data.
- **The camera stays on your device.** Camera frames used to scan your cube
  are processed in real time on your phone and are **never uploaded, saved
  or shared**.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Settings, lesson progress, streaks
  and timer results are stored locally and are removed when you uninstall
  the app.
- **Ads are provided by Google AdMob.** AdMob may collect device identifiers
  and usage data to show and measure ads, subject to your consent where the
  law requires it.

## 1. Information processed on your device only

| Data | Purpose | Leaves your device? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera frames | Reading the sticker colors of your cube while you scan it | **No.** Frames are analyzed in memory and discarded immediately. No photos or videos are stored. |
| Language, sound/haptics and animation preferences | Remembering your settings | No |
| Academy progress, unlocked lessons, daily-challenge streak, timer history | Showing your progress | No |

We cannot see, access or recover any of this information. Clearing the
app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

### Camera permission

The app asks for camera access only when you open the scanner. You can
decline and enter cube colors by hand, or revoke the permission at any time
in your device settings.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of CubeMentor shows a banner ad and optional rewarded
video ads (for example, to unlock advanced lessons). Ads are served by
**Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited.

To serve, personalize (where permitted), measure and protect ads against
fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

- device identifiers such as the **Android Advertising ID** or the **iOS
  Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)**, and app-set identifiers;
- IP address (used to derive approximate location);
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, app
  version) and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, crashes).

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- AdMob and user data: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

We do not receive personal information from Google that identifies you.

### Your ad choices and consent

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR) and US states with privacy laws:** on
  first launch the app shows Google's consent message (User Messaging
  Platform). You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes. Ads that do not
  rely on personal data may still be shown if you refuse personalization.
- **Change your choice at any time** in the app: *Settings → Privacy & ads*
  (shown in regions where this choice is required).
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in
  *Settings → Google → Ads* (or *Privacy → Ads*).
- **iOS:** the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)
  before the IDFA is used; you can change it in
  *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*.

If no ad can be loaded (for example, when you are offline), the app still
works, and features gated by rewarded ads are unlocked for that session.

## 3. Feedback email

If you choose to send feedback from *Settings*, the app opens your own email
app with our address filled in. We receive only what you decide to send
(your email address and message) and use it solely to reply and improve
the app. We do not add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete
your messages at any time.

## 4. Children

CubeMentor is a general-audience puzzle app and is not directed at children
under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us
personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The only third
party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, as described in
section 2.

## 6. Security and retention

Data stored by the app stays in the app's private storage on your device
and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Feedback emails are kept only as
long as needed to handle your request.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct,
delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw
consent. Because we keep no account data on servers, most of these rights
are exercised directly in the app (Settings → Privacy & ads, Settings →
Reset progress) or in your device settings. For anything else, email us.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish
the new version at the same address. Significant changes will be noted in
the app's release notes.
